Are You Experiencing Identity Aging?

Dr. Michael Levin at Tufts University discovered that organisms don't age primarily because of DNA damage. They age when their bioelectric patterns degrade - when they lose the informational "map" telling cells what to maintain.

His most radical hypothesis: organisms age when they complete their developmental goals and have no new challenges. He calls this "boredom death."

The Key Insight:

Planarian flatworms are functionally immortal. They escape aging by constantly tearing in half and regenerating - giving cells perpetual developmental challenges. Purpose prevents decay.

This lab applies Levin's framework to you. Your identity is also a bioelectric pattern - what we call your ATCF (Adaptive Temporal Coherence Function).

This 15-minute assessment will measure your identity coherence across four dimensions and detect if you're experiencing boredom death.

What You'll Discover:

  • • Your A-score (Autobiographical Continuity): Connection to your past
  • • Your T-score (Temporal Integration): Present-moment capacity
  • • Your C-score (Prospective Coherence): Future horizons and hope
  • • Your F-score (Meta-Constructive Capacity): Ability to learn and adapt

The Boredom Death Pattern:

High A (solid past, achievements) + Low C (no compelling future) + Low F (not growing) = Your identity is degrading

If detected, you'll receive evidence-based "anthrobot protocols" - regenerative challenges proven to reverse the pattern.

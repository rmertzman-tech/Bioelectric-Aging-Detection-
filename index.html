Plain Language Summary

This assessment measures whether your sense of identity is "aging" like cells age.

  • Time: 15-20 minutes
  • Questions: 20 questions about your life and goals
  • Privacy: Your answers are anonymous unless you choose to share
  • Purpose: Learn if you need new life goals to stay mentally healthy

The Thermodynamics of Identity Aging

Your identity is not just psychological—it's physical. It obeys the laws of thermodynamics.

Two Forces Shape Your Identity:

🔥 FIRE

Active Inference - The metabolic work of creating meaning. Your brain burns glucose to run simulations, update beliefs, and change the world.

❄️ ICE

Logical Depth - The frozen work of your past. Memories, skills, values crystallized into organized patterns.

The Fourth Law:

"OSCILLATE OR DIE"

If you stop alternating between exploration and consolidation, your identity ages.

What This Assessment Measures:

A - Autobiographical Continuity

Your accumulated past achievements and memories.

Plain language: How connected you feel to your past self.

T - Temporal Integration

Current mental energy available for thinking clearly.

Plain language: How much mental energy you have right now.

C - Prospective Coherence

Future-directed goals that pull you forward.

Plain language: Whether you have exciting goals for your future.

F - Meta-Constructive Capacity

Ability to learn and adapt to new situations.

Plain language: How easily you can learn new things.
